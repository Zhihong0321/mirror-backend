What happens if it is left: If it lapses, mail to eternalgy@atap.solar bounces and any site or service on the domain drops: quotes, invoices and tender correspondence stop reaching the company without warning.

What we saw: The domain atap.solar, which carries the published address eternalgy@atap.solar, is registered only until 4 December 2026 and has under 30 days left at the time of the audit.
