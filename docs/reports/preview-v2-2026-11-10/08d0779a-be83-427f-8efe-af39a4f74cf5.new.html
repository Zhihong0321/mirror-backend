The atap.solar domain expires on 4 December 2026 and has no transfer lock

The domain behind the company's published email address, eternalgy@atap.solar, expires on 2026-12-04, and its registry status does not include clientTransferProhibited.

Why it matters: Losing atap.solar kills eternalgy@atap.solar — the address printed on the official site — so quotes, invoices and tender correspondence stop arriving, and any file hosted there goes dark; an unlocked domain can also be transferred away by an attacker.
